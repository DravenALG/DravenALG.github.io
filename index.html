A little about me

Biography.

I am currently a PhD Student at MMLab@NTU, College of Computing and Data Science in Nanyang Technological University, Singapore, supervised by Prof. Chen Change Loy.

I earned my M.Eng. degree in Sun Yat-sen University, advised by Prof. Wei-Shi Zheng, where I develop strong research skills and gain valuable research experience, and also enjoy a wonderful and happy time. Additionally, I was a visiting scholar in Macquarie University during my Master‘s time, advised by Prof. Longbing Cao.

I obtained my B.Eng. degree in Shandong University. At that time, I had my first attempt on research and cultivated the interest in it, advised by Prof. Xin-Shun Xu and Associate Prof. Xin Luo.

Looking ahead

Research interests.

I am interested in Deep Learning, Computer Vision and Embodied AI.

Now I am mainly working on Robotics Agentic Harness, Vision-Language-Action Models (VLA) and World Action Models (WAM), hoping to build general-purpose, efficient Robotic Foundation Models (RFMs) in the future.
