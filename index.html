About me

I’m a PhD student at MMLab@NTU in the College of Computing and Data Science at Nanyang Technological University, Singapore. My advisor is Prof. Chen Change Loy.

I earned my M.Eng. at Sun Yat-sen University with Prof. Wei-Shi Zheng. During my master’s, I also spent time as a visiting scholar at Macquarie University with Prof. Longbing Cao.

I got my start in research during my B.Eng. at Shandong University, working with Prof. Xin-Shun Xu and Associate Prof. Xin Luo.

Research

I’m interested in deep learning, computer vision and embodied AI. I work on robotic harnesses, vision-language-action models (VLAs) and world action models (WAMs). My goal is to build efficient, general-purpose robotic foundation models.
